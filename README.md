# Privacy Policy — Rivens to Endo

Effective Date: April 12, 2026

## Overview

Rivens to Endo is committed to protecting your privacy. This app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Ever.

## Data Collection

 We collect no data from you. There are no user accounts, no registration, no login, and no forms that submit your information anywhere.

## Network Requests

The app fetches publicly available riven mod listing data from the Warframe Market public API (warframe.market). These are standard HTTP requests that do not include any personal information about you.

## Local Storage

Some preferences and cached data (such as your saved settings and filters) are stored locally on your device using your device's built-in storage. This data:

- Never leaves your device
- - Is never transmitted to any server
- Is never shared with any third party
- Can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app

## Push Notifications

If you enable notifications, the app schedules them locally on your device. No notification tokens or id
entifiers are sent to any external server.

## Third Parties

This app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting services, or tr
acking tools.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated policy will be posted here with a new effective date. Given that this app collects no data, we do not anticipate meaningful changes.
